06 — Questions

Before you book.

Which session should I start with?

If you're not sure, book the free 15-minute discovery call. If you already have an interview coming up, go straight to Interview Prep & Tips or a Mock Interview.

What should I prepare?

Bring your résumé, a link to the project or paper you want to discuss, and two or three specific questions. Concrete material makes a short session much more useful.

Can you run a session for my college or club?

Yes. Group talks and hands-on workshops are arranged directly rather than through Topmate. See past sessions on the Speaking page and get in touch through the contact page.

Where do I pay and reschedule?

Everything is handled on Topmate: booking, payment, reminders and changes. This website only links to it.
