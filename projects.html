Oct 2026

NovaMart Multi-Agent E-commerce RAG

A supervisor agent on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore that sends each question to inventory, policy, refund and communication agents. The policy agent searches three Bedrock knowledge bases (S3 Vectors) at once, and all agents share a versioned DynamoDB state table. Capstone of the Future AWS Agent Engineer Nanodegree; scored 120/120 on the official tests.
